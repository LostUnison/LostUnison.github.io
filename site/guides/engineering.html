    Engineering Bonus

    Item Attachment

    Helm Cloak Gloves Belt Boots
    Helm – Mind Amplification Dish: +45 Stamina + mind control.

    Cloak – Flexweave Underlay: +23 Agility + parachute.

    Cloak – Springy Arachnoweave: +27 Spell power + parachute.

    Gloves – Hyperspeed Accelerators: 340 Haste for 12s (1m CD).

    Gloves – Reticulated Armor Webbing: 885 Armor

    		 Belt – Frag Belt: Extra bomb ability (does stack with Eternal Belt Buckle). Boots – Nitro Boosts: +24 Crit + sprint (can backfire).

    Usually, if you are a tank, you can apply the Helm – Mind Amplification Dish and Gloves – Reticulated Armor Webbing.

    Some classes would rather the 20 haste enchant over Cloak – Flexweave Underlay or Cloak – Springy Arachnoweave.

    Belt – Frag Belt can be applied along with Eternal Belt Buckle.

    Casters and melee would use Gloves – Hyperspeed Accelerators.

    And finally, Boots – Nitro Boosts should always be prioritized.

    Make Gold with Engineering

    Engineering is probably not the best profession for gold making

    Nonetheless, here’s the craft I recommend.

    Summary

    Method Item/Service Why It Sells Profit Style
    Consumables Saronite Bombs, Global Thermal Sapper Charges Raiders & PvPers need extra DPS tools High-volume, steady
    Ammo Iceblade Arrows, Shatter Rounds BiS ranged ammo for hunters Constant demand
    Weapon Enchants Heartseeker Scope (+40 Crit), Sun Scope (+40 Haste) Best ranged enchants Medium, steady
    Companion Pets Pet Bombling, Lil’ Smoky Collectors & casual players Niche, slow but good margins
    Luxury Mounts Mechano-Hog / Mekgineer’s Chopper Prestige mount, high-ticket sales Big profit, big investment
    Crafted Gear Early crafted goggles & items Fresh 80s gearing up Early-expansion only
    Utility Bots MOLL-E, Jeeves Mail, repair, bank access Service-based gold (tips/fees)
    Vanity Items Fireworks, toys, fun gadgets RP & casual markets Niche sales

    Consumables

    Saronite bombs are obtained through Box of Bombs.

    Ammo

    These crafts require recipes obtained inside Icecrown Citadel.

    • You must reach Honored reputation with the Ashen Verdict to purchase them.

    • Each recipe costs 1 Primordial Saronite.

    • Recipes are tied to specific Engineering specializations (Gnomish or Goblin).

    Recommendation:
    For long-term usefulness in the later patches of WotLK, go for Iceblade Arrows as a Gnomish Engineer. They remain in high demand, especially for hunters, making them the most profitable choice.

    Mount

    The recipe is obtained through a vendor

    • Horde Vendor: Requires Exalted with the Horde Expedition

    • Alliance Vendor: Requires Exalted with the Alliance Vanguard
