    Stoneguard Band Disenchanting

    Step 1: Get 390 Jewelcrafting and 375+ Enchanting

    Step 2: Buy Eternal Earth and Crystallized Earth from AH

    Buy this item For this price in AH (single item)
    Eternal Earth 125 maximum
    Crystallized Earth (turn 10 into Eternal Earth) 1.5g maximum
    Stoneguard Band

    Price may differ depending on the market, usually don’t go over 125% of the disenchant value of Stoneguard Band.

    Exemple:

    Disenchant value = 30g, Max Eternal Earth price to buy = (30 * 125%)/2 = 37.5 / 2 = 18.5g

    Step 3: Craft Stoneguard Band with Jewelcrafting and disenchant them

    Step 4: Read the Enchanting guide and make enchant scrolls

    Step 5: Sell enchant scrolls in AH

    Step 6: Repeat
