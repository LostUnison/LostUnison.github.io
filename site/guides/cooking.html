    Cooking Award

    To obtain Dalaran Cooking Award, you must complete daily cooking quests, each quest awards 2 Dalaran Cooking Awards.

    Learn Northern Stew

    Learn Northern Stew by completing a quest, Northern Stew is used in one of the daily cooking quests.

    Bring 4x Chilled Meat Chilled Meat to any one of the Grand Master Cooking Trainer in the following faction settlements:

    Northern Cooking Northern Cooking - Brom Brewbaster, Howling Fjord, Valgarde, /way 58.2 62.0

    Northern Cooking Northern Cooking - Rollick MacKreel, Borean Tundra, Valiance Keep, /way 57.8 71.4

    Northern Cooking Northern Cooking - Thomas Kolichio, Howling Fjord, Vengeance Landing (outside), /way 78.6 29.4

    Northern Cooking Northern Cooking - Orn Tenderhoof, Borean Tundra, Warsong Hold, /way 42.0 54.2

    Daily Quests

    Requirements to open Cooking dailies:

    Minimum character level: 65

    Minimum Cooking skill: 350

    Daily cooking quests are a great way to earn some gold and cooking awards.

    For more informations about the quests and preparation visit this guide: https://www.wowhead.com/wotlk/guide/professions/cooking/daily-quests
