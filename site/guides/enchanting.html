    Enchanting Bonuses

    1. Profession Perk (Self-Only Enchants)

    As an Enchanter, you can enchant your own rings - this is the main profession bonus.

    Ring Enchants (Exclusive to Enchanters)

    Enchant Effect Notes
    Enchant Ring - Greater Assault +40 Attack Power per ring Total: +80 AP
    Enchant Ring - Greater Spellpower +23 Spell Power per ring Total: +46 SP
    Enchant Ring - Stamina +30 Stamina per ring Total: +60 Stamina

    2. Regular Enchanting Benefits

    Besides the self-only perks, you can also:

    • Disenchant items into dusts, essences, and shards (used for enchanting or selling).

    • Enchant weapons and armor for other players (a steady gold source).

    Make Gold with Enchanting

    Vellums

    To create enchanted scrolls, you’ll need Weapon and Armor Vellums.
    These items are made by Scribes through the Inscription profession,
    but you can easily buy them on the Auction House if you don’t have Inscription yourself.

    Vellums are divided into three tiers:

    • The first tier is used for Classic enchants,

    • The second tier is for TBC enchants,

    • And the third tier is for WotLK enchants.

    Once you have the right vellum, simply cast your enchant directly onto it -
    This turns it into an Enchanting Scroll that you can then post and sell on the Auction House.
