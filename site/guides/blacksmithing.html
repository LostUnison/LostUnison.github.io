    Blacksmithing Bonus

    At level 400 Blacksmithing, you unlock Socket Bracer and Socket Gloves. Enabling to add 1 additional gem to your bracers and gloves.

    Keep in mind the bonus will become disabled if you unlearn Blacksmithing,

    How to make gold with Blacksmithing

    Rods

    Adamantite and Eternium rod requires recipes (available in the Auction House)

    Gear

    264 Item Level

    Recipe vendor: Alchemist Finklestein in Icecrown Citadel (raid).

    Requires to be Revered with the Ashen Verdict to get all the recipes (some of them only require Honored).

    245 Item Level

    Recipes drop in Trial of the Crusader (ToC raid) or you can buy them in the Auction House.

    Each item has a Horde and Alliance version.

    232 Item Level

    Recipes drop in Ulduar (raid) or you can buy them in the Auction House.

    213 Item Level

    200 Item Level

    Item Attachments

    Titanium Plating is learnt through a plan.

    Titanium Plating Recipe

    • Horde Vendor: Requires Exalted with the Horde Expedition

    • Alliance Vendor: Requires Exalted with the Alliance Vanguard
