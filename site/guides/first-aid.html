    WotLK Warmane Icecrown - First Aid 1 to 450 (x3)

    Leveling guide by Lost Unison

    First Aid is a secondary profession, so every character can learn it next to two primary professions. It only costs cloth and trainer fees, and the last bandage heals 5,800 in 8 seconds.

    • Type Secondary profession
    • Rate x3 (about 3 points per craft)
    • Materials Cloth only
    • Hardest step Heavy Frostweave manual

    1. Required materials
    2. Leveling 1 to 450
    3. Manual: Heavy Frostweave Bandage

    Required materials

    • Linen Cloth41
    • Wool Cloth35
    • Silk Cloth30
    • Mageweave Cloth24
    • Runecloth16
    • Netherweave Cloth30
    • Frostweave Cloth47
    • Manual: Heavy Frostweave Bandage1

    Most bandages are already yellow when you learn them, and the last steps turn green. Yellow and green crafts don't always give points, so you will probably need more than listed.

    Buy 3 to 5 extra of each cloth, and about 15 extra Frostweave Cloth.

    Manual: Heavy Frostweave Bandage

    The manual is a world drop and binds when picked up, so you can't buy it on the Auction House.

    • Drop condition: it only drops once your First Aid is at least 390. You need 400 to learn it.
    • Where: humanoids and undead in Zul'Drak, Icecrown and The Storm Peaks, and level 80 dungeons.
    • Best farm: Rageclaw Primalists and Drakuru Berserkers in Zul'Drak, near Light's Breach.

    Tip: reach 390 before you go farming, then finish 390 to 400 with Frostweave Bandages while you wait for the drop.

    Bonuses

    • Heavy Frostweave Bandage: heals 5,800 damage over 8 seconds. Useful when leveling, soloing and in PvP.
    • Achievement Grand Master Medic: reach 450 First Aid.
    • Achievement Stocking Up: create 500 Heavy Frostweave Bandages.
