    WotLK Warmane Icecrown - Ashen Verdict Reputation Farm

    Guide by Lost Unison · Last updated 2026-11-19

    Why farm the Ashen Verdict, requirements, how the farm works and rewards.

    On this page
    1. Introduction
    2. Why farm it
    3. Requirements
    4. How the farm works
    5. Rewards

    Nov 14, 2026

    Introduction

    The Ashen Verdict is the Icecrown Citadel faction: reaching Exalted needs about 42,000 reputation, and the fastest known route is repeated trash pulls in 10-man ICC, with no boss kills.

    Why farm it

    Exalted with the Ashen Verdict gives you a role ring of item level 277, and Honored to Revered opens profession recipes sold in Light's Hammer.

    Once you are Friendly, Ormus the Penitent gives the quest "Choose Your Path". You pick one of five rings, one per role:

    Ring Role
    Ashen Band of Courage Tank
    Ashen Band of Wisdom Healer
    Ashen Band of Destruction Caster DPS
    Ashen Band of Vengeance Agility DPS
    Ashen Band of Might Strength DPS

    The recipe vendor is Alchemist Finklestein in Light's Hammer. Each recipe costs 1 Primordial Saronite, and it covers Blacksmithing, Leatherworking, Tailoring and Engineering ammo.

    Requirements

    You need a full 10-man group in a raid group, set to the 10-man Icecrown Citadel difficulty.

    • 10 players in a raid group, ideally with a tank, a healer and strong AoE damage

    • Gear good enough to survive large pulls without wipes

    • Food and flasks if your group struggles with the big mobs

    A stronger group clears pulls faster, and speed is what decides how long the farm takes.

    How the farm works

    You kill only the trash in the first area, before Lord Marrowgar, and you never kill a boss.

    1. Enter Icecrown Citadel 10 with your raid group.

    2. Clear the trash packs before Lord Marrowgar, pull by pull.

    3. Do not kill Lord Marrowgar or any other boss, so the instance is not saved to you.

    4. Leave the instance and reset it.

    5. Enter again and repeat.

    Since no boss dies, you are not locked to the instance and you can reset it as often as the server allows.

    Standing Reputation needed for this step
    Friendly 3,000
    Honored 6,000
    Revered 12,000
    Exalted 21,000

    Rewards

    Each standing unlocks something, and the Exalted ring is the main prize [VERIFY the standing for each item on Warmane].

    Standing Reward
    Friendly Quest "Choose Your Path" from Ormus the Penitent, to pick your role ring
    Honored Some profession recipes, plus Engineering ammo recipes
    Revered Blacksmithing, Leatherworking and Tailoring recipes
    Exalted Role ring upgraded to item level 277
